## The 30-day rule

> SCENE (list): What a shop must show.
> The price today | the sale price @ announced
> The lowest price in the last 30 days | next to every announced cut @ thirty days
> Since 2022 | German price-labelling rules @ twenty twenty-two

[calm] Since twenty twenty-two, a shop announcing a price cut must also show the lowest price it charged in the thirty days before.

> SCENE (receipt): The law's words.
> bg | bf-bg-law
> Preisangabenverordnung, § 11 | The rule
> ==Lowest total price== | within the last 30 days @ lowest total price
> shown with every announced price cut | @ announced

[calm] That's the law. The lowest total price, shown next to every announced cut.

> SCENE (photo): The history.
> bf-history | Same headphones, 10% cheaper than last month | color

[amused] Picture Mia opening the history. Same headphones, only ten percent cheaper than last month. Her minus forty was minus ten.

> SCENE (receipt): One gap.
> bg | bf-bg-gap
> PAngV § 11, paragraph 4 | Not covered
> ==Personal prices== | coupon codes and individual deals @ like a coupon
> Fast-spoiling food | marked down before it goes off @ shown to everyone

[calm] One gap. Personal deals, like a coupon code, aren't covered. The rule is about prices shown to everyone.

## Sending it back

> SCENE (photo): The parcel.
> bf-parcel | Online: 14 days, no reason | color

[curious] Mia buys a different pair. The box arrives, and they don't fit. Can she send them back? Online, yes. Fourteen days from delivery, no reason needed.

> SCENE (receipt): In a shop.
> bg | bf-bg-shop
> Verbraucherzentrale | In a shop
> ==No automatic right== | to exchange what you just changed your mind about @ seller
> Many shops offer it anyway | check the receipt @ favour

[calm] In a shop it's different. A seller doesn't have to take back what you simply changed your mind about. Many do, as a favour. Check the receipt.

> SCENE (list): Guarantee.
> A sale changes nothing | the guarantee stays @ sale
> Two years | legal guarantee: repair or replace, reduced or not @ hairdryer

[calm] And a sale never cancels the guarantee. If the hairdryer breaks, the seller owes you a fix or a swap for two years, reduced or not.
