## The courts

{Q80}

> SCENE (question): Question card #80. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Welches Gericht in Deutschland ist zuständig für die Auslegung des Grundgesetzes?]] Which court interprets the Basic Law?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Bundesverfassungsgericht]], the Federal Constitutional Court in Karlsruhe. It's the only court that can declare a law unconstitutional.

{Q139}

> SCENE (question): Question card #139. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wann kommt es in Deutschland zu einem Prozess vor Gericht? Wenn jemand …]] When does a case go to court? When someone…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[eine Straftat begangen hat und angeklagt wird.]] has committed a crime and been charged. Not for changing religion, not for disagreeing with the government. And parking in the wrong place gets you a fine and a towed car, not a trial.

{Q146}

> SCENE (question): Question card #146. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wie nennt man in Deutschland ein Verfahren vor einem Gericht?]] What is a court case called?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Prozess]]. Not [[Prozedur]], not [[Protokoll]]. They sound similar, which is exactly why they're there.

{Q142}

> SCENE (question): Question card #142. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was ist die Hauptaufgabe eines Richters in Deutschland?]] What is a judge's main task?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[arbeitet an einem Gericht und spricht Urteile.]] Works at a court and delivers judgments. Representing people in court is the lawyer's job, and changing laws is parliament's.

{Q147}

> SCENE (question): Question card #147. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was ist die Arbeit eines Richters in Deutschland?]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Recht sprechen]]. Literally, "speaking the law".

> SCENE (term): Three questions, one answer.
> Judikative | the judiciary: die rechtsprechende Gewalt

[dramatic] Three questions ask the same thing: which power does a judge belong to? Learn the answer in both languages.

{Q143}

> SCENE (question): Question card #143. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Eine Richterin oder ein Richter in Deutschland gehört zur …]] A judge belongs to the…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Judikative.]] The judiciary. [[Operative]] isn't one of the three powers at all.

{Q144}

> SCENE (question): Question card #144. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Eine Richterin oder ein Richter gehört in Deutschland zur …]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[rechtsprechenden Gewalt.]] The judicial power. The [[vollziehende Gewalt]] is the executive, the [[gesetzgebende Gewalt]] the legislature, and [[planende Gewalt]] doesn't exist.

{Q145}

> SCENE (question): Question card #145. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] And the third version: [[In Deutschland wird die Staatsgewalt geteilt. Für welche Staatsgewalt arbeitet ein Richter?]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Judikative]]. And as you know, the press is not a state power.

## The people in the courtroom

{Q140}

> SCENE (question): Question card #140. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ über Schuld und Strafe

[dramatic] [[Was macht ein Schöffe in Deutschland?]] What does a lay judge, a [[Schöffe]], do?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[entscheidet mit Richtern über Schuld und Strafe.]] Decides on guilt and sentence, together with the professional judges. Ordinary citizens, chosen for five years, with a full vote.

{Q150}

> SCENE (question): Question card #150. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ ehrenamtlicher Richter

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Eine Gerichtsschöffin oder ein Gerichtsschöffe in Deutschland ist …]] A lay judge is… an [[ehrenamtlicher Richter]], an honorary judge. Unpaid, apart from expenses, and they don't need to have studied law.

{Q141}

> SCENE (question): Question card #141. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ ein Rechtsanwalt

[dramatic] [[Wer berät in Deutschland Personen bei Rechtsfragen und vertritt sie vor Gericht?]] Who advises people on legal questions and represents them in court?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[ein Rechtsanwalt]], a lawyer. The [[Staatsanwalt]] is the prosecutor: the one who accuses on behalf of the state.

## Work, family and shopping

{Q137}

> SCENE (question): Question card #137. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] Germany also has special courts. [[Welches Gericht ist in Deutschland bei Konflikten in der Arbeitswelt zuständig?]] Which court handles conflicts at work?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[das Arbeitsgericht]], the labour court.

{Q136}

> SCENE (question): Question card #136. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ ungerechtfertigter Kündigung

[dramatic] [[Sie gehen in Deutschland zum Arbeitsgericht bei …]] You go to the labour court when…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[ungerechtfertigter Kündigung]]. A wrong service-charge bill, trouble with neighbours, a car accident: those go elsewhere.

{Q138}

> SCENE (question): Question card #138. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was kann ich in Deutschland machen, wenn mir mein Arbeitgeber zu Unrecht gekündigt hat?]] What can you do if you were fired unfairly?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Kündigungsschutzklage erheben]], file a claim for unfair dismissal. And be quick: you usually have three weeks. Going to the police won't help; a dismissal isn't a crime.

{Q263}

> SCENE (question): Question card #263. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland sind Jugendliche ab 14 Jahren strafmündig. Das bedeutet: Jugendliche, die 14 Jahre und älter sind und gegen Strafgesetze verstoßen, …]] From fourteen, young people are criminally responsible. That means they…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[werden bestraft.]] Can be punished. But not like adults: there's a separate youth criminal law, focused on education rather than punishment. And parents don't share the sentence.

{Q267}

> SCENE (question): Question card #267. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Eine junge Frau in Deutschland, 22 Jahre alt, lebt mit ihrem Freund zusammen. Die Eltern der Frau finden das nicht gut, weil ihnen der Freund nicht gefällt. Was können die Eltern tun?]] A twenty-two-year-old lives with her boyfriend; her parents don't like him. What can they do?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Sie müssen die Entscheidung der volljährigen Tochter respektieren.]] Respect her decision. She's an adult. They can't bring her home, report her to the police, or choose another man for her.

{Q254}

> SCENE (question): Question card #254. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland dürfen Ehepaare sich scheiden lassen. Meistens müssen sie dazu das "Trennungsjahr" einhalten. Was bedeutet das?]] What is the "separation year" before a divorce?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Die Ehegatten führen mindestens ein Jahr getrennt ihr eigenes Leben. Danach ist die Scheidung möglich.]] The couple live separate lives for at least a year; then they can divorce.

{Q275}

> SCENE (question): Question card #275. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ die Unterstützung einer Anwältin

[dramatic] [[Was braucht man in Deutschland für eine Ehescheidung?]] What do you need for a divorce?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[die Unterstützung einer Anwältin]] or a lawyer. The divorce has to be filed by a lawyer. Not your parents' consent, not your children's, not a doctor's note.

{Q256}

> SCENE (question): Question card #256. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Ein Ehepaar möchte in Deutschland ein Restaurant eröffnen. Was braucht es dazu unbedingt?]] A couple want to open a restaurant. What do they need?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[eine Gaststättenerlaubnis von der zuständigen Behörde]], a restaurant licence from the responsible authority. Not the police, not a party, not the registration office.

{Q262}

> SCENE (question): Question card #262. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was bedeutet in Deutschland der Grundsatz der Gleichbehandlung?]] What does the principle of equal treatment mean?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Niemand darf z.B. wegen einer Behinderung benachteiligt werden.]] Nobody may be put at a disadvantage, for example because of a disability. Article 3 of the Basic Law says exactly that.

{Q290}

> SCENE (question): Question card #290. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Sie haben in Deutschland einen Fernseher gekauft. Zu Hause packen Sie den Fernseher aus, doch er funktioniert nicht. Der Fernseher ist kaputt. Was können Sie machen?]] Your new TV doesn't work. What can you do?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[den Fernseher reklamieren]], take it back and make a claim. The shop has to repair or replace it: for two years, the law protects you against faulty goods. You don't report it to the police, and you don't swap it without asking.

## What to remember for the exam

> SCENE (list): Recap board. Three cards appear one by one.
> Judikative | → judges, rechtsprechende Gewalt @ One.
> Schöffe | → an unpaid judge, with a full vote @ Two.
> Arbeitsgericht | → unfair dismissal: three weeks @ Three.

[dramatic] Three things to take away. One: a judge belongs to the [[Judikative]], the judicial power. Their job: [[Recht sprechen]].

[dramatic] Two: a [[Schöffe]] is an ordinary citizen who judges, unpaid, with a full vote.

[dramatic] Three: trouble at work goes to the [[Arbeitsgericht]]. And for an unfair dismissal, act within three weeks.
