## Cold open

> SCENE (list): Who elects whom.
> Volk | elects the Bundestag @ The people
> Bundestag | elects the chancellor @ the chancellor
> Bundesversammlung | elects the president @ the president

[dramatic] Here's a chain worth memorising. The people elect the Bundestag. The Bundestag elects the chancellor. And a special assembly, the Bundesversammlung, elects the president. Nobody in Germany votes for the chancellor directly.

[dramatic] This lesson is about the government, the Bundesrat, and the last few questions on democracy. Twenty of them.

> SCENE (title): Title card.
> WAHLEN & PARTEIEN · TEIL 3

## The chancellor and the president

{Q81}

> SCENE (question): Question card #81. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wer wählt den Bundeskanzler in Deutschland?]] Who elects the chancellor?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[der Bundestag]]. Not the people, and not the Bundesrat.

{Q83}

> SCENE (question): Question card #83. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] The same question again: [[Wer wählt den deutschen Bundeskanzler?]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[der Bundestag]]. The trap here is [[die Bundesversammlung]]: that one elects the president.

{Q82}

> SCENE (question): Question card #82. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ den Bundeskanzler

[dramatic] [[Wer leitet das deutsche Bundeskabinett?]] Who leads the federal cabinet?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[den Bundeskanzler]]. The chancellor sets the guidelines of policy; the ministers run their departments within them.

{Q87}

> SCENE (question): Question card #87. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ der Bundespräsident

[dramatic] [[Wer ist das Staatsoberhaupt der Bundesrepublik Deutschland?]] Who is the head of state?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[der Bundespräsident]], the federal president. The chancellor leads the government, but the president is head of state.

{Q70}

> SCENE (question): Question card #70. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ schlägt den Kanzler zur Wahl vor

[dramatic] [[Was gehört zu den Aufgaben des deutschen Bundespräsidenten?]] The exam shows President Gustav Heinemann handing Helmut Schmidt his certificate of appointment as chancellor, in 1974. What is one of the president's tasks?

[countdown] [dramatic] The president [[schlägt den Kanzler zur Wahl vor]]: proposes the chancellor for election by the Bundestag, and then appoints them. The president doesn't run the government or pick the ministers.

{Q65}

> SCENE (question): Question card #65. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Es gehört nicht zu den Aufgaben des Deutschen Bundestages, …]] What is not a task of the Bundestag?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[das Bundeskabinett zu bilden.]] To form the cabinet. That's the chancellor's job. The Bundestag drafts laws, controls the government, and elects the chancellor.

{Q71}

> SCENE (question): Question card #71. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ in Berlin

[dramatic] [[Wo hält sich der deutsche Bundeskanzler am häufigsten auf?]] Where is the chancellor most of the time?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[in Berlin]], because the Chancellery and the Bundestag are there. Not in Bonn, which was the capital until 1990 and the seat of government until 1999. And not at Schloss Meseberg, which is only the government's guest house. Some study materials get this one wrong: the answer is Berlin.

## The Bundesrat

> SCENE (states): The sixteen states, each with a voice in the Bundesrat.
> ALLE | Der Bundesrat: 16 Länder @ sixteen states

[dramatic] The states have their own say in federal laws, through the [[Bundesrat]]. All sixteen states sit in it.

{Q85}

> SCENE (question): Question card #85. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wer bildet den deutschen Bundesrat?]] Who makes up the Bundesrat?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[die Regierungsvertreter der Bundesländer]], representatives of the state governments. Not elected members of parliament: ministers sent by each state government.

{Q90}

> SCENE (question): Question card #90. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Die deutschen Bundesländer wirken an der Gesetzgebung des Bundes mit durch …]] The states take part in federal law-making through…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[den Bundesrat.]]

{Q91}

> SCENE (question): Question card #91. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland kann ein Regierungswechsel in einem Bundesland Auswirkungen auf die Bundespolitik haben. Das Regieren wird …]] A change of government in one state can affect federal politics. Governing becomes…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[schwieriger, wenn dadurch die Mehrheit im Bundesrat verändert wird.]] Harder, if it changes the majority in the Bundesrat. Many laws need the Bundesrat's approval, so state elections matter for Berlin too.

{Q60}

> SCENE (question): Question card #60. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland gehören der Bundestag und der Bundesrat zur …]] The Bundestag and the Bundesrat belong to the…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Legislative.]] The law-making power.

## What kind of state

{Q26}

> SCENE (question): Question card #26. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Deutschland ist …]] Germany is…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[ein demokratischer und sozialer Bundesstaat.]] A democratic and social federal state. Those are the exact words of Article 20 of the Basic Law.

{Q38}

> SCENE (question): Question card #38. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] And the fill-in-the-blank version: [[Die Bundesrepublik Deutschland ist ein demokratischer und sozialer …]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Bundesstaat.]] Not a [[Staatenbund]], which is a looser alliance of states, and not a [[Zentralstaat]].

{Q158}

> SCENE (question): Question card #158. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Das "Dritte Reich" war eine …]] The "Third Reich" was a…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Diktatur.]] A dictatorship. Everything in this lesson, free elections, parties, opposition, was abolished in 1933.

{Q216}

> SCENE (question): Question card #216. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Welches Symbol ist im Plenarsaal des Deutschen Bundestages zu sehen?]] The exam shows the Bundestag's plenary chamber. Which symbol hangs there?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[der Bundesadler]], the federal eagle. Not the imperial eagle or the imperial crown.

## Democracy close to home

{Q131}

> SCENE (question): Question card #131. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland ist ein Bürgermeister …]] A mayor is…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[das Oberhaupt einer Gemeinde.]] The head of a town or municipality.

{Q95}

> SCENE (question): Question card #95. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was gilt für die meisten Kinder in Deutschland?]] What applies to most children?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Schulpflicht]], compulsory schooling. There's no compulsory voting, and no compulsory religion.

{Q260}

> SCENE (question): Question card #260. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland hat ein Kind in der Schule …]] At school, a child has…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Anwesenheitspflicht.]] A duty to attend. No unlimited free time, and no free choice of every subject.

{Q250}

> SCENE (question): Question card #250. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland hat man die besten Chancen auf einen gut bezahlten Arbeitsplatz, wenn man …]] Your best chance of a well-paid job is if you…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[gut ausgebildet ist.]] are well trained. Not your religion, your gender or your party.

{Q268}

> SCENE (question): Question card #268. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Eine junge Frau will den Führerschein machen. Sie hat Angst vor der Prüfung, weil ihre Muttersprache nicht Deutsch ist. Was ist richtig?]] A young woman wants a driving licence, but German isn't her first language. What's right?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Sie kann die Theorie-Prüfung vielleicht in ihrer Muttersprache machen. Es gibt mehr als zehn Sprachen zur Auswahl.]] She can probably take the theory test in her own language: there are more than ten to choose from.

## What to remember for the exam

> SCENE (list): Recap board. Three cards appear one by one.
> Volk → Bundestag → Kanzler | → who elects whom @ One.
> Bundesrat | → the states' governments @ Two.
> demokratischer und sozialer Bundesstaat | → Article 20 @ Three.

[dramatic] Three things to take away. One: the people elect the Bundestag, the Bundestag elects the chancellor, the Bundesversammlung elects the president.

[dramatic] Two: the Bundesrat is made of state governments, and gives the states a say in federal laws.

[dramatic] Three: Germany is a democratic and social federal state.

> SCENE (words): The words appear one by one, then settle into a grid.
> Bundeskanzler, Bundespräsident, Bundeskabinett, Bundesrat, Legislative, Bundesstaat, Bürgermeister, Schulpflicht

[dramatic] And the words to recognise on the day: [[Bundeskanzler]]. [[Bundespräsident]]. [[Bundeskabinett]]. [[Bundesrat]]. [[Legislative]]. [[Bundesstaat]]. [[Bürgermeister]]. [[Schulpflicht]].
