## Cold open

> SCENE (stat): Election day, in numbers.
> 18 | the voting age @ eighteen
> 4 | years between elections @ every four years

[curious] Every four years, on a Sunday, Germans go to a school gym or a town hall, step behind a curtain, and put two crosses on a long sheet of paper. That's a federal election.

[warm] Once you're a citizen, that will be you. This lesson covers how voting works: who can vote, how, and how often. Twenty exam questions, and the first part of a three-part lesson on elections and parties.

## Who votes

{Q28}

> SCENE (question): Question card #28.

[[Wer wählt in Deutschland die Abgeordneten zum Bundestag?]] Who elects the members of the Bundestag? [[das wahlberechtigte Volk]], the people who have the right to vote. Not the army, not business, not the civil service.

{Q94}

> SCENE (question): Question card #94.

[[Ab welchem Alter darf man in Deutschland an der Wahl zum Deutschen Bundestag teilnehmen?]] From what age can you vote in a Bundestag election? [[18]]. Some states let sixteen-year-olds vote in their own elections, and the European election starts at sixteen. But the Bundestag: eighteen.

{Q108}

> SCENE (question): Question card #108.
> @ mindestens 18 Jahre

[[Bei einer Bundestagswahl in Deutschland darf jede oder jeder wählen, die oder der …]] Who may vote in a Bundestag election?

A German citizen who is [[mindestens 18 Jahre]] old. Living here isn't enough, however long. That's one of the things citizenship will give you.

{Q114}

> SCENE (question): Question card #114.

[[An demokratischen Wahlen in Deutschland teilzunehmen ist …]] Taking part in elections is… [[ein Recht.]] A right. Not a duty: nobody is fined for not voting.

{Q115}

> SCENE (question): Question card #115.

[[Was bedeutet "aktives Wahlrecht" in Deutschland?]] What is "active" voting right? [[Man kann wählen.]] You can vote. Being able to be elected is the "passive" right.

{Q116}

> SCENE (question): Question card #116.

[[Wenn Sie bei einer Bundestagswahl in Deutschland wählen dürfen, heißt das …]] If you're allowed to vote, that's called… [[aktives Wahlrecht.]] Not a campaign, not a procedure.

## How you vote

> SCENE (list): Three words for every German election.
> frei | nobody forces you @ free
> gleich | every vote counts the same @ equal
> geheim | nobody sees your cross @ secret

[warm] German elections follow a few principles. Three of them are in the exam: free, equal and secret.

{Q112}

> SCENE (question): Question card #112.

[[Die Wahlen in Deutschland sind …]] Elections in Germany are… [[geheim.]] Secret. That's why there's a booth, and why you fold your ballot.

{Q122}

> SCENE (question): Question card #122.

[[Welchem Grundsatz unterliegen Wahlen in Deutschland? Wahlen in Deutschland sind …]] Which principles? [[frei, gleich, geheim.]] Free, equal, secret. The traps mix in "open" and "safe". Elections are never "open": your vote is private.

{Q126}

> SCENE (question): Question card #126.

[[Was bekommen wahlberechtigte Bürgerinnen und Bürger in Deutschland vor einer Wahl?]] What do voters receive before an election?

[[eine Wahlbenachrichtigung von der Gemeinde]], a polling card from your town. It tells you where your polling station is. You don't need anyone's permission to vote.

{Q133}

> SCENE (question): Question card #133.

[[Was ist bei Bundestags- und Landtagswahlen in Deutschland erlaubt?]] What is allowed in federal and state elections?

[[Man kann durch Briefwahl seine Stimme abgeben.]] Voting by post. Not by phone, not a husband voting for his wife, and not children from fourteen.

{Q130}

> SCENE (question): Question card #130. The four ballots; the valid one lights up.
> @ one cross on each side

[[Welcher Stimmzettel wäre bei einer Bundestagswahl gültig?]] Which ballot paper would be valid?

The exam shows four ballots. The valid one has one cross on each side: one for the first vote, one for the second. Two crosses in the same column make that vote invalid.

## Counting the votes

{Q105}

> SCENE (question): Question card #105.

[[Was ist eine Aufgabe von Wahlhelferinnen und Wahlhelfern in Deutschland?]] What do election workers do?

[[Sie zählen die Stimmen nach dem Ende der Wahl.]] They count the votes when the polls close. They don't help people inside the booth, and they don't send early results to the media.

{Q106}

> SCENE (question): Question card #106.

Same question, second version: [[Sie zählen die Stimmen nach dem Ende der Wahl.]] They count the votes.

{Q282}

> SCENE (question): Question card #282.
> @ Wahlhelfer

[[Welches Ehrenamt müssen deutsche Staatsbürgerinnen und Staatsbürger übernehmen, wenn sie dazu aufgefordert werden?]] Which unpaid role do citizens have to accept if asked?

[[Wahlhelfer]], election worker. You can only refuse for a good reason. Coaching a club or supervising a library is voluntary.

## How often

{Q107}

> SCENE (question): Question card #107.

[[Für wie viele Jahre wird der Bundestag in Deutschland gewählt?]] For how many years is the Bundestag elected? [[4 Jahre]].

{Q109}

> SCENE (question): Question card #109.

[[Wie oft gibt es normalerweise Bundestagswahlen in Deutschland?]] How often are there federal elections? [[alle vier Jahre]], every four years. "Normally", because if a government falls, there can be an early election, as in February 2025.

{Q110}

> SCENE (question): Question card #110.

And the same question with different numbers: [[4 Jahre]] again. Most state parliaments sit for five years, but the Bundestag for four.

{Q124}

> SCENE (question): Question card #124.

[[Die Bundestagswahl in Deutschland ist die Wahl …]] The Bundestag election elects… [[des Parlaments für Deutschland.]] The national parliament. Not the chancellor, and not the president.

{Q129}

> SCENE (question): Question card #129.

[[Vom Volk gewählt wird in Deutschland …]] What do the people elect directly? [[der Bundestag.]] The chancellor is elected by the Bundestag, the president by the Bundesversammlung, a state premier by the state parliament.

{Q125}

> SCENE (question): Question card #125.

[[In einer Demokratie ist eine Funktion von regelmäßigen Wahlen, …]] One function of regular elections is…

[[nach dem Willen der Wählermehrheit den Wechsel der Regierung zu ermöglichen.]] To allow a change of government, when the majority of voters wants one. That's the heart of democracy: power on loan, for four years at a time.

## What to remember for the exam

> SCENE (list): Recap board. Three cards appear one by one.
> frei, gleich, geheim | → the principles @ One.
> 18 · 4 Jahre | → voting age, Bundestag term @ Two.
> Wahlhelfer | → count the votes @ Three.

[warm] Three things to take away. One: elections are free, equal and secret.

Two: you vote for the Bundestag from eighteen, every four years.

Three: election workers count the votes, and citizens have to help if they're asked.
