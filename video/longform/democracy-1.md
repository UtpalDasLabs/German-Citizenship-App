## Cold open

> SCENE (stat): Election day, in numbers.
> 18 | the voting age @ eighteen
> 4 | years between elections @ every four years

[dramatic] Every four years, on a Sunday, Germans go to a school gym or a town hall, step behind a curtain, and put two crosses on a long sheet of paper. That's a federal election.

[dramatic] Once you're a citizen, that will be you. This lesson covers how voting works: who can vote, how, and how often. Twenty exam questions, and the first part of a three-part lesson on elections and parties.

## Who votes

{Q28}

> SCENE (question): Question card #28. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wer wählt in Deutschland die Abgeordneten zum Bundestag?]] Who elects the members of the Bundestag?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[das wahlberechtigte Volk]], the people who have the right to vote. Not the army, not business, not the civil service.

{Q94}

> SCENE (question): Question card #94. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Ab welchem Alter darf man in Deutschland an der Wahl zum Deutschen Bundestag teilnehmen?]] From what age can you vote in a Bundestag election?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[18]]. Some states let sixteen-year-olds vote in their own elections, and the European election starts at sixteen. But the Bundestag: eighteen.

{Q108}

> SCENE (question): Question card #108. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ mindestens 18 Jahre

[dramatic] [[Bei einer Bundestagswahl in Deutschland darf jede oder jeder wählen, die oder der …]] Who may vote in a Bundestag election?

[countdown] [dramatic] A German citizen who is [[mindestens 18 Jahre]] old. Living here isn't enough, however long. That's one of the things citizenship will give you.

{Q114}

> SCENE (question): Question card #114. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[An demokratischen Wahlen in Deutschland teilzunehmen ist …]] Taking part in elections is…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[ein Recht.]] A right. Not a duty: nobody is fined for not voting.

{Q115}

> SCENE (question): Question card #115. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was bedeutet "aktives Wahlrecht" in Deutschland?]] What is "active" voting right?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Man kann wählen.]] You can vote. Being able to be elected is the "passive" right.

{Q116}

> SCENE (question): Question card #116. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wenn Sie bei einer Bundestagswahl in Deutschland wählen dürfen, heißt das …]] If you're allowed to vote, that's called…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[aktives Wahlrecht.]] Not a campaign, not a procedure.

## How you vote

> SCENE (list): Three words for every German election.
> frei | nobody forces you @ free
> gleich | every vote counts the same @ equal
> geheim | nobody sees your cross @ secret

[dramatic] German elections follow a few principles. Three of them are in the exam: free, equal and secret.

{Q112}

> SCENE (question): Question card #112. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Die Wahlen in Deutschland sind …]] Elections in Germany are…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[geheim.]] Secret. That's why there's a booth, and why you fold your ballot.

{Q122}

> SCENE (question): Question card #122. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Welchem Grundsatz unterliegen Wahlen in Deutschland? Wahlen in Deutschland sind …]] Which principles?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[frei, gleich, geheim.]] Free, equal, secret. The traps mix in "open" and "safe". Elections are never "open": your vote is private.

{Q126}

> SCENE (question): Question card #126. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was bekommen wahlberechtigte Bürgerinnen und Bürger in Deutschland vor einer Wahl?]] What do voters receive before an election?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[eine Wahlbenachrichtigung von der Gemeinde]], a polling card from your town. It tells you where your polling station is. You don't need anyone's permission to vote.

{Q133}

> SCENE (question): Question card #133. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was ist bei Bundestags- und Landtagswahlen in Deutschland erlaubt?]] What is allowed in federal and state elections?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Man kann durch Briefwahl seine Stimme abgeben.]] Voting by post. Not by phone, not a husband voting for his wife, and not children from fourteen.

{Q130}

> SCENE (question): Question card #130. The four ballots; the valid one lights up. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ one cross on each side

[dramatic] [[Welcher Stimmzettel wäre bei einer Bundestagswahl gültig?]] Which ballot paper would be valid?

[dramatic] The exam shows four ballots.

[countdown] [dramatic] The valid one has one cross on each side: one for the first vote, one for the second. Two crosses in the same column make that vote invalid.

## Counting the votes

{Q105}

> SCENE (question): Question card #105. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was ist eine Aufgabe von Wahlhelferinnen und Wahlhelfern in Deutschland?]] What do election workers do?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Sie zählen die Stimmen nach dem Ende der Wahl.]] They count the votes when the polls close. They don't help people inside the booth, and they don't send early results to the media.

{Q106}

> SCENE (question): Question card #106. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Sie zählen die Stimmen nach dem Ende der Wahl.]] They count the votes.

{Q282}

> SCENE (question): Question card #282. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ Wahlhelfer

[dramatic] [[Welches Ehrenamt müssen deutsche Staatsbürgerinnen und Staatsbürger übernehmen, wenn sie dazu aufgefordert werden?]] Which unpaid role do citizens have to accept if asked?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Wahlhelfer]], election worker. You can only refuse for a good reason. Coaching a club or supervising a library is voluntary.

## How often

{Q107}

> SCENE (question): Question card #107. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Für wie viele Jahre wird der Bundestag in Deutschland gewählt?]] For how many years is the Bundestag elected?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[4 Jahre]].

{Q109}

> SCENE (question): Question card #109. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wie oft gibt es normalerweise Bundestagswahlen in Deutschland?]] How often are there federal elections?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[alle vier Jahre]], every four years. "Normally", because if a government falls, there can be an early election, as in February 2025.

{Q110}

> SCENE (question): Question card #110. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[countdown] [dramatic] [[4 Jahre]] again. Most state parliaments sit for five years, but the Bundestag for four.

{Q124}

> SCENE (question): Question card #124. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Die Bundestagswahl in Deutschland ist die Wahl …]] The Bundestag election elects…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[des Parlaments für Deutschland.]] The national parliament. Not the chancellor, and not the president.

{Q129}

> SCENE (question): Question card #129. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Vom Volk gewählt wird in Deutschland …]] What do the people elect directly?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[der Bundestag.]] The chancellor is elected by the Bundestag, the president by the Bundesversammlung, a state premier by the state parliament.

{Q125}

> SCENE (question): Question card #125. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In einer Demokratie ist eine Funktion von regelmäßigen Wahlen, …]] One function of regular elections is…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[nach dem Willen der Wählermehrheit den Wechsel der Regierung zu ermöglichen.]] To allow a change of government, when the majority of voters wants one. That's the heart of democracy: power on loan, for four years at a time.
