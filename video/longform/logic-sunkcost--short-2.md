> SCENE (theatre): Three price tickets drawn at random: $15, $2 off, $7 off.

[calm] Same plays, same seats, different price. Who showed up more?

[calm] A university theatre was selling season tickets. As people came to the window, the seller handed each of them, by lottery, one of three prices. Full price, fifteen dollars. Two dollars off. Or seven dollars off. Same plays. Same seats. Only the price was luck.

[calm] If money already spent shouldn't matter, all three groups should go to the theatre equally often. They didn't.

> SCENE (theatre2): Bars: full price 4.1 tickets used, discounts 3.3 and 3.3.

[warmly] Over the first five plays, the full-price group used just over four tickets each. The discount groups used just over three. The more people had paid, the more they showed up. Not because the plays were better. Because they had paid.
