## The döner rule

> SCENE (photo): The spit.
> doener-hook | Europe nearly decided what a döner may be | color

[curious] In 2025, Europe nearly decided what a döner may be. The veal and turkey döner common in Germany would not have qualified.

> SCENE (term): The label.
> Traditional Speciality Guaranteed | an EU label that fixes a recipe, not a place | Examples: Mozzarella, Pizza Napoletana

[calm] The tool was an EU label called Traditional Speciality Guaranteed. It doesn't protect a place. It fixes a recipe, like the one for Neapolitan pizza.

> SCENE (photo): The applicant.
> spit-stock | Udofed, Ankara, 2022 | color

[calm] The applicant was Udofed, an international döner association based in Ankara. It applied in 2022. And its rules were strict.

> SCENE (bignums): The rules.
> The proposed rules (chicken: only breast or thigh)
> 16 months | beef, at least @ sixteen months
> 6 months | lamb, at least @ six months

[calm] Beef only from cattle at least sixteen months old. Lamb at least six months. Chicken, only breast or thigh.

> SCENE (stamp): No veal, no turkey, no veggie.
> No veal. No turkey. No veggie. | red

[amused] No veal. No turkey. No vegetable döner.

> SCENE (receipt): The objections.
> bg | bg-typewriter
> ZDFheute, 26 September 2025 | Who objected
> Germany's ==Federal Agriculture Ministry== | objected @ agriculture ministry
> The hotel and restaurant association ==DEHOGA== | objected @ DEHOGA
> A ==Turkish döner makers' association== in Europe | objected @ Turkish döner makers

[calm] Germany's agriculture ministry objected. So did DEHOGA, the hotel and restaurant association. And so did a Turkish döner makers' association in Europe.

> SCENE (term): The application's own words.
> A cultural symbol | of Turkish immigration to Germany | The application's description of the döner (dpa via ZDFheute)

[calm] The application itself noted it: the döner had become a cultural symbol of Turkish immigration to Germany.

> SCENE (photo): Withdrawn.
> night-street | Withdrawn on 23 September 2025 | color

[warmly] On 23 September 2025, the application was withdrawn. Nobody official said why. EU sources say it may have wanted to avoid a rejection.

## The Currywurst paper trail

> SCENE (line): Who invented the Currywurst?
> Who invented the Currywurst?

[curious] Now the other icon. Who invented the Currywurst?

> SCENE (photo): The story.
> currywurst-hook | Berlin, 4 September 1949 | color

[warmly] The story goes: Berlin, 4 September 1949. Herta Heuwer, at her snack stand, mixes tomato paste, curry powder and Worcestershire sauce.

> SCENE (receipt): The trade mark.
> bg | bg-typewriter2
> German Patent and Trade Mark Office | Trade mark no. 721319
> Filed 21 February 1958: ==Chillup== | probably chilli plus ketchup @ Chillup
> Registered 21 January 1959 for ==special sauce== | a trade mark protects a name @ special sauce

[calm] Her paper trail: a trade mark. In 1958 she filed the name Chillup with Germany's patent office, for a special sauce. A trade mark protects a name.

> SCENE (stamp): Not a patent.
> Not a patent | red

[calm] Not a patent.

> SCENE (photo): The secret sauce.
> sauce-stock | She kept the recipe secret until 1999 | color

[calm] A patent would have meant publishing the recipe. She kept it secret until she died in 1999.

> SCENE (term): Curry.
> Curry | from the Tamil word kari: sauce | So Currywurst is sauce sausage (DWDS)

[curious] Word lab. Curry comes, through English, from the Tamil word kari: sauce. So Currywurst is, quite literally, sauce sausage.

> SCENE (receipt): Hamburg.
> bg | bg-archive
> DPMA, The Spirit of Currywurst | Hamburg's claim
> A plaque at Großneumarkt, 2003, honours ==Lena Brücker== | a character in Uwe Timm's novel @ Lena Brücker
> She is ==only a fictitious character== | said the DPMA @ She never existed

[calm] Hamburg claims it too. A 2003 plaque there honours Lena Brücker, a character in Uwe Timm's novel. She never existed.

> SCENE (photo): The dispute.
> wurst-stock | A national dispute | color

[amused] A national dispute settled by a fictional woman.

> SCENE (receipt): The office's answer.
> bg | bg-berlin
> German Patent and Trade Mark Office | Who invented the sausage with sauce?
> ==We don't know.== | the office's own answer @ we don't know

[calm] So who did invent it? Germany's patent and trade mark office asked itself exactly that. Its own answer: we don't know.

> SCENE (term): The word itself.
> Currywurst | refused as a trade mark | Too common: everyone should be free to use it (DPMA)

[calm] And the word Currywurst itself can't be owned. The office refused it: too common, and everyone should be free to use it.
