## Cold open

> SCENE (stat): Two votes on one ballot.
> 1 | Erststimme: a person @ first vote
> 2 | Zweitstimme: a party @ second vote

[dramatic] On a German ballot you have two votes. The first, the [[Erststimme]], is for a person: the candidate for your area. The second, the [[Zweitstimme]], is for a party. And the second one decides how many seats each party gets.

[dramatic] This lesson follows those votes into the Bundestag: parties, the five percent hurdle, coalitions, government and opposition. Twenty more questions.

## Why parties

{Q41}

> SCENE (question): Question card #41. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Warum gibt es in einer Demokratie mehr als eine Partei?]] Why is there more than one party in a democracy?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[weil dadurch die unterschiedlichen Meinungen der Bürgerinnen und Bürger vertreten werden]]. Because citizens have different opinions, and each deserves a voice. It's not about competition in business, and not about stopping demonstrations.

{Q93}

> SCENE (question): Question card #93. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Je mehr "Zweitstimmen" eine Partei bei einer Bundestagswahl bekommt, desto …]] The more second votes a party gets, the…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[mehr Sitze erhält die Partei im Parlament.]] The more seats it gets in parliament. That's why the second vote matters most.

{Q113}

> SCENE (question): Question card #113. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wahlen in Deutschland gewinnt die Partei, die …]] The party that wins an election is the one that…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[die meisten Stimmen bekommt.]] gets the most votes. Not the most votes from men, or from workers. Every vote counts the same.

## The five percent hurdle

> SCENE (stat): The hurdle.
> 5 % | of second votes to enter the Bundestag @ five percent

[dramatic] There's one big rule for getting into the Bundestag at all: a party needs at least five percent of the second votes.

{Q117}

> SCENE (question): Question card #117. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wie viel Prozent der Zweitstimmen müssen Parteien mindestens bekommen, um in den Deutschen Bundestag gewählt zu werden?]] How many percent?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[5%]]. There are narrow exceptions, for parties that win three constituencies and for national minorities, but for the exam: five.

{Q121}

> SCENE (question): Question card #121. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Eine Partei möchte in den Deutschen Bundestag. Sie muss aber einen Mindestanteil an Wählerstimmen haben. Das heißt …]] The minimum share of votes is called…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[5%-Hürde.]] The five percent hurdle.

{Q123}

> SCENE (question): Question card #123. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Was ist in Deutschland die "5%-Hürde"?]] What is it?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Mindestanteil an Wählerstimmen, um ins Parlament zu kommen]]. The minimum share of votes to get into parliament. It has nothing to do with attendance.

{Q127}

> SCENE (question): Question card #127. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Warum gibt es die 5%-Hürde im Wahlgesetz der Bundesrepublik Deutschland? Es gibt sie, weil …]] Why does it exist?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[viele kleine Parteien die Regierungsbildung erschweren.]] Because many small parties make it hard to form a government. The Weimar Republic had dozens of parties in parliament, and governments kept collapsing. The hurdle is a lesson from that.

{Q120}

> SCENE (question): Question card #120. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Das Wahlsystem in Deutschland ist ein …]] Germany's electoral system is a…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Mehrheits- und Verhältniswahlrecht.]] A mix of majority and proportional voting: the first vote picks a person by majority, the second shares out seats by proportion. The other options are historical, unequal systems.

## Coalitions

{Q31}

> SCENE (question): Question card #31. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Die Zusammenarbeit von Parteien zur Bildung einer Regierung nennt man in Deutschland …]] When parties work together to form a government, it's called a…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Koalition.]] Coalition. A [[Fraktion]] is something else: we'll get to that.

{Q103}

> SCENE (question): Question card #103. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ von SPD, FDP

[dramatic] [[Was wird in Deutschland als "Ampelkoalition" bezeichnet?]] What is a "traffic light coalition"?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[von SPD, FDP und den Grünen in einer Regierung]]. Red for the SPD, yellow for the FDP, green for the Greens: like a traffic light. That coalition governed from 2021 to 2024.

{Q73}

> SCENE (question): Question card #73. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ CDU und CSU

[dramatic] [[Die beiden größten Fraktionen im Deutschen Bundestag heißen zurzeit …]] The two largest groups in the Bundestag at the moment?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[CDU und CSU, und die AfD.]] That's the result of the February 2025 election. If there's a new election, this answer can change.

## Inside the Bundestag

{Q13}

> SCENE (question): Question card #13. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ alle Abgeordneten, die nicht

[dramatic] [[Im Parlament steht der Begriff "Opposition" für …]] In parliament, the "opposition" means…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[alle Abgeordneten, die nicht zu der Regierungspartei oder den Regierungsparteien gehören.]] All the members who don't belong to the governing parties.

{Q88}

> SCENE (question): Question card #88. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Die parlamentarische Opposition im Deutschen Bundestag …]] What does the opposition do?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[kontrolliert die Regierung.]] It holds the government to account: asks questions, criticises, offers alternatives.

{Q89}

> SCENE (question): Question card #89. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wie nennt man in Deutschland die Vereinigung von Abgeordneten einer Partei im Parlament?]] What's the group of one party's members in parliament called?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Fraktion]]. A parliamentary group. Not to be confused with a coalition, which is between parties.

{Q98}

> SCENE (question): Question card #98. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wenn Abgeordnete im Deutschen Bundestag ihre Fraktion wechseln, …]] If members switch parliamentary groups…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[kann die Regierung ihre Mehrheit verlieren.]] The government can lose its majority. Members of the Bundestag are free to do that: they answer to their conscience, not to the party.

{Q57}

> SCENE (question): Question card #57. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ ein Abgeordneter der stärksten

[dramatic] [[Wer wird meistens zur Präsidentin oder zum Präsidenten des Deutschen Bundestages gewählt?]] Who usually becomes President of the Bundestag?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[ein Abgeordneter der stärksten Fraktion]]: a member of the largest parliamentary group. Not the oldest member, and not a former chancellor.

{Q128}

> SCENE (question): Question card #128. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Parlamentsmitglieder, die von den Bürgerinnen und Bürgern gewählt werden, nennt man …]] Members of parliament elected by the people are called…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Abgeordnete.]] Not ambassadors, not chancellors.

## What to remember for the exam

> SCENE (list): Recap board. Three cards appear one by one.
> Zweitstimme | → decides the seats @ One.
> 5%-Hürde | → keeps parliament workable @ Two.
> Koalition, Fraktion | → between parties, within one @ Three.

[dramatic] Three things to take away. One: the second vote decides how many seats a party gets.

[dramatic] Two: below five percent, a party usually doesn't get in, so that parliament can form governments.

[dramatic] Three: a coalition is between parties; a [[Fraktion]] is the group within one.

> SCENE (words): The words appear one by one, then settle into a grid.

[dramatic] And the words to recognise on the day: [[Zweitstimme]]. [[5%-Hürde]]. [[Koalition]]. [[Ampelkoalition]]. [[Opposition]]. [[Fraktion]]. [[Abgeordnete]]. [[Bundestag]].
