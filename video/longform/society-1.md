## Children and parents

{Q248}

> SCENE (question): Question card #248. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Die Erziehung der Kinder ist in Deutschland vor allem Aufgabe …]] Raising children in Germany is mainly the task of…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[der Eltern.]] The parents. The Basic Law says it directly, in Article 6: the care and upbringing of children is the natural right of parents, and their first duty.

{Q249}

> SCENE (question): Question card #249. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] And the same question, worded differently: [[Wer ist in Deutschland hauptsächlich verantwortlich für die Kindererziehung?]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[die Eltern]]. Not the state, not the school, not the grandparents.

{Q242}

> SCENE (question): Question card #242. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ die Eltern entscheiden

[dramatic] [[Wer entscheidet, ob ein Kind in Deutschland in den Kindergarten geht?]] Who decides whether a child goes to kindergarten?

[dramatic] Again: the parents.

[countdown] [dramatic] [[die Eltern entscheiden]]. Kindergarten is an offer, not an obligation.

{Q269}

> SCENE (question): Question card #269. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] But it's an offer you have a right to. [[In Deutschland haben Kinder ab dem Alter von drei Jahren bis zur Ersteinschulung einen Anspruch auf …]] From the age of three until they start school, children have a right to…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[einen Kindergartenplatz.]] A kindergarten place. If your town can't offer one, it's breaking the law.

{Q251}

> SCENE (question): Question card #251. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] Parents decide a lot, but not everything. [[Wenn man in Deutschland ein Kind schlägt, …]] If you hit a child in Germany…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[kann man dafür bestraft werden.]] You can be punished for it. Since 2000, the law says children have a right to an upbringing without violence. It's not only the family's business.

{Q255}

> SCENE (question): Question card #255. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] And if parents are struggling, they can get help. [[Bei Erziehungsproblemen können Eltern in Deutschland Hilfe erhalten vom …]] Parents with parenting problems can get help from the…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Jugendamt.]] The youth welfare office. Not the health department, not the school office. The [[Jugendamt]] isn't only there to step in when things go wrong; most of its work is advice and support.

{Q273}

> SCENE (question): Question card #273. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] Same answer, different words: [[Bei Erziehungsproblemen gehen Sie in Deutschland …]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[zum Jugendamt.]]

## School and learning

{Q2}

> SCENE (question): Question card #2. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland können Eltern bis zum 14. Lebensjahr ihres Kindes entscheiden, ob es in der Schule am …]] Until a child is fourteen, parents can decide whether it takes part in…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Religionsunterricht teilnimmt.]] Religious education. At fourteen, the child decides for itself. History, politics and languages are compulsory; religion is the one subject families can opt out of.

{Q244}

> SCENE (question): Question card #244. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Welchen Schulabschluss braucht man normalerweise, um an einer Universität in Deutschland ein Studium zu beginnen?]] Which school qualification do you usually need to go to university?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[das Abitur]]. The final exam at a [[Gymnasium]]. A [[Gesellenprüfung]] ends an apprenticeship, and [[Prokura]] isn't a qualification at all: it's a power of attorney in a company.

{Q257}

> SCENE (question): Question card #257. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Eine erwachsene Frau möchte in Deutschland das Abitur nachholen. Das kann sie an …]] An adult woman wants to take her Abitur later. Where?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[einem Abendgymnasium.]] An evening school for adults.

{Q261}

> SCENE (question): Question card #261. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] And the man version: [[Ein Mann möchte mit 30 Jahren in Deutschland sein Abitur nachholen. Wo kann er das tun? An …]]

[countdown] [dramatic] [[einem Abendgymnasium.]] Not a university: you need the Abitur to get in there.

{Q270}

> SCENE (question): Question card #270. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Die Volkshochschule in Deutschland ist eine Einrichtung …]] The Volkshochschule is a place…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[zur Weiterbildung.]] For further education, for everyone: languages, computers, cooking, and very often the German and integration courses that people take before this exam.

## Adults, marriage and partners

{Q246}

> SCENE (question): Question card #246. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Ab welchem Alter ist man in Deutschland volljährig?]] At what age are you an adult?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[18]].

{Q245}

> SCENE (question): Question card #245. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ Anne, thirteen

[dramatic] [[Wer darf in Deutschland nicht als Paar zusammenleben?]] Who may not live together as a couple?

[countdown] [dramatic] Anne, thirteen, and Tim, twenty-five. A thirteen-year-old is a child, and the law protects children. The other couples are all fine: two young adults, two men, two women. The age gap between Tom and Klaus doesn't matter; they're both adults.

{Q247}

> SCENE (question): Question card #247. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Eine Frau ist schwanger. Sie ist kurz vor und nach der Geburt ihres Kindes vom Gesetz besonders beschützt. Wie heißt dieser Schutz?]] A pregnant woman is specially protected just before and after the birth. What is that called?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Mutterschutz]], maternity protection. Six weeks before the birth and eight weeks after, she doesn't have to work. Don't mix it up with [[Elternzeit]], parental leave, which either parent can take later.

{Q252}

> SCENE (question): Question card #252. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.
> @ nur mit einem Partner

[dramatic] [[In Deutschland …]] In Germany…

[countdown] [dramatic] [[nur mit einem Partner]]. You can remarry after a divorce, and a widow can remarry too.

{Q272}

> SCENE (question): Question card #272. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] And the [[nicht]] version: [[Welche Lebensform ist in Deutschland nicht erlaubt?]] Which way of living is not allowed?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Ein Mann ist mit zwei Frauen zur selben Zeit verheiratet.]] A man married to two women at the same time. Two women living together, a divorced couple with new partners, a single father: all perfectly normal. Since 2017, two women or two men can also marry.

## Living with the neighbours

{Q266}

> SCENE (question): Question card #266. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Wann beginnt die gesetzliche Nachtruhe in Deutschland?]] When does the legal quiet time at night begin?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[um 22 Uhr]], at ten p.m. Not at sunset, not when your neighbours go to bed. From ten at night until six in the morning, keep the noise down.

{Q279}

> SCENE (question): Question card #279. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[In den meisten Mietshäusern in Deutschland gibt es eine "Hausordnung". Was steht in einer solchen "Hausordnung"? Sie nennt …]] Most rented buildings have a "Hausordnung". What's in it?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Regeln, an die sich alle Bewohnerinnen und Bewohner halten müssen.]] Rules everyone in the building has to follow: quiet times, cleaning the stairs, where the bins go. It's not a list of tenants, and it has nothing to do with buses.

{Q243}

> SCENE (question): Question card #243. Three seconds to guess, then the answer highlights.

[dramatic] [[Maik und Sybille wollen mit Freunden an ihrem deutschen Wohnort eine Demonstration auf der Straße abhalten. Was müssen sie vorher tun?]] Maik and Sybille want to hold a demonstration in the street. What do they have to do first?

[countdown] [dramatic] [[Sie müssen die Demonstration anmelden.]] Register it with the authorities, usually at least forty-eight hours before announcing it. That's not asking permission: the right to demonstrate is a basic right. It's so the police can close the road and keep everyone safe.
