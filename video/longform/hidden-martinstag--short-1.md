[calm] Why do kids in Germany walk through the dark with lanterns every November?

[calm] Today, the [[Laternenumzug]], the lantern procession, is the part most people see. Children make their own lanterns, often at kindergarten or school, and walk together after dark, singing songs like [[Laterne, Laterne]]. In many towns, someone dressed as Martin rides ahead on a horse.
