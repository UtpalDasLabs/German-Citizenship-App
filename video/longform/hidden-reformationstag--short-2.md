[calm] You've probably seen the picture: Martin Luther, hammer in hand, nailing ninety-five theses to the church door in Wittenberg, on the thirty-first of October, 1517.

> SCENE (photo): The church today; an old church door.
> schlosskirche |  | color
> church-door |  | color @ no eyewitness

[calm] But historians still argue about whether it happened. Luther himself never mentions it in his writings, and no eyewitness from the time describes it.

> SCENE (photo): A quill and a letter by candlelight.
> quill |  | color

[calm] What we do know: on that same day, he sent the theses with a letter to the Archbishop of Mainz. Soon they were printed, and they spread fast.

> SCENE (photo): The theses door again.
> thesentuer |  | color

[warmly] So the hammer may be a legend. The letter is history. And that's why the thirty-first of October is [[Reformationstag]].
